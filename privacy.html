This Privacy Policy is from Fryntavo Games ("we", "us"). It applies to all mobile games and apps we publish on Google Play under the developer name Fryntavo Games, and to this website.

  • We do not ask for your name, address or phone number.

5. HOW WE USE DATA

  • To run the games and their features
  • To show ads, which keep our games free
  • To understand how the games are played and make them better
  • To fix crashes and bugs
  • To answer support requests
  • To follow the law and prevent fraud or cheating

6. SHARING

We share data only with the service providers listed in section 4, and with authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

7. ADS AND YOUR CHOICES

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings: Settings > Google > Ads.
  • You can turn off personalised ads in the same place.
  • Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice in the game settings.

8. PERMISSIONS

Our games ask only for the permissions they need, such as internet access to load ads and save progress. Each game's Google Play page lists its permissions.

9. CHILDREN

Our games are not directed at children under 13 (or the age set by the law in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. KEEPING AND DELETING DATA

We keep data only as long as we need it for the reasons in this policy. Analytics and crash data are stored by our providers for their standard retention period and then deleted or made anonymous.

You can ask us to delete your data at any time. Go to Delete My Data for the steps. We may keep a small amount of data when the law requires it, or to prevent fraud.

11. SECURITY

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We work with trusted providers and limit access to data. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable care.

13. DATA IN OTHER COUNTRIES

Our providers may process data on servers outside your country. They use legal safeguards for such transfers.
