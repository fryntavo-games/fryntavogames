DELETE MY DATA

Account and data deletion for all Fryntavo Games titles

HOW TO REQUEST DELETION

This page is for players of any game published by Fryntavo Games on Google Play. You do not need to have the game installed.

  1. Send an email to support@yourdomain.com with the subject Delete My Data.
  2. Tell us the name of the game.
  3. Add your Player ID or account email if the game has one (find it in the game's Settings screen).
  4. We confirm your request and delete your data within 30 days.

SEND DELETE REQUEST

WHAT WE DELETE

  • Your game account, if the game has accounts
  • Saved progress and scores stored on our servers
  • Analytics data linked to your ID
  • Your support emails

WHAT MAY BE KEPT

  • Purchase records, which we must keep for tax and legal reasons, for as long as the law requires
  • Data needed to prevent fraud or cheating, for up to 90 days
  • Anonymous statistics that cannot be linked to you

DATA ON YOUR DEVICE

Progress saved only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game, or when you go to Android Settings > Apps > (game name) > Storage > Clear data.

ADVERTISING ID

To reset or delete your Advertising ID, go to Android Settings > Google > Ads.
