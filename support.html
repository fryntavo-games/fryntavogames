COMMON QUESTIONS

THE GAME CRASHES OR WILL NOT OPEN

Update the game from Google Play, restart your phone, and check you have free storage space. If it still fails, email us your device model and Android version.

I LOST MY PROGRESS

Progress may be saved on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data can remove it. Email us and we will try to help.

A PURCHASE DID NOT ARRIVE

Restart the game while online. If the item is still missing, email us the order number from your Google Play receipt.

HOW DO I GET A REFUND?

Refunds are handled by Google Play. Open Google Play, go to your order history and request a refund there.

HOW DO I DELETE MY DATA?

Follow the steps on our Delete My Data page.

WHY ARE THERE ADS?

Ads keep our games free to play. You can turn off personalised ads in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
